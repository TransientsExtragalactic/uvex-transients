LogisticSampler#

class uvex_transients.phase_space.sampling.logistic.LogisticSampler(space: Space, center: Sequence[float], slope: Sequence[float])[source]#

Succeed with probability \(p(x)=\mathrm{expit}[\sum_i s_i (x_i - c_i)]\).

The boundary \(p=1/2\) is the plane through center that is perpendicular to slope, and slope sets how sharp it is.

Parameters:

  • space (Space) – The axes of the experiment.

  • center (sequence of float) – A point on the boundary, one coordinate per axis.

  • slope (sequence of float) – How fast the logit of the probability rises along each axis.

Raises:

ValueError – If center or slope does not have one entry per axis.
