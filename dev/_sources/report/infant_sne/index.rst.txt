*******************************
Infant Supernovae
*******************************

.. card::
   :class-card: sd-shadow-sm gallery-banner

   *Run:* ``configs/infant_sne.yaml`` · *Data:* ``get_results(..., simulation="infant_sne")``

   Every supernova class, sampled only over the 72 hours after explosion, with a loose 23 AB
   magnitude screen and a 5 sigma SNR cut. The brighter selection needed for spectroscopic
   follow-up is applied to the event summary table afterwards.

   :ref:`Back to the report <report_page>`
