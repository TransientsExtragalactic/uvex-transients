**********************************
Target of Opportunity Simulations
**********************************

.. card::
   :class-card: sd-shadow-sm gallery-banner

   *Module:* :mod:`~uvex_transients.models.core`

   Simulate one specific, already-known event pointed at directly, on whatever cadence is
   chosen: no schedule, no windowed sampling, no
   :class:`~uvex_transients.simulation.event.Event`.
